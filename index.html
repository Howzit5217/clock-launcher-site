Everything a desk clock should be

Simple to look at. Lots to love.

Move the mouse and a slim bar appears; leave it and only the time is left. Everything else is in Settings, laid out like Windows 11, with a search box.

30 themes, 10 styles

Digital, flip, analog, segment, binary, words and more, with 11 ways for the digits to change.

Living scenes

Forest, ocean, sunset, space and more, with the real moon phase, and birds that carry in each new minute.

Your own picture

Any photo, any size: fill, fit, stretch or tile it, zoom it and drag it into place. The colors can match it by themselves.

See-through

Let your desktop show through, clear or frosted like glass, so it is just the time floating on your screen.

Alarms, timer, stopwatch

Alarms ring even with the clock hidden, start gently and can wake the PC from sleep.

Always on the right screen

It opens on the display it was last on, waits for screens that wake up late, and moves back when one reconnects.

Pin it on top

One click keeps it above every other window, even the ones you open after it.

Sips power

Windows efficiency mode, a pause whenever nobody can see it, and a gentle start that never slows down signing in.

Keeps itself up to date

New versions come from this page by themselves, while you are not using the clock. Or choose to be asked first.

30 themes

Find one that feels like you

Every theme, showing the time right now. Pick one to try it above. In the app you can also change the font, the colors and the background, and save your favorite combinations.

For Windows 10 and 11

Download ClockLauncher.exe All versions
SHA-256 fingerprint, to check the download is the real one
  1. Download ClockLauncher.exe and double-click it.
  2. If Windows says it protected your PC, choose More info, then Run anyway. That's because the app isn't code-signed, which costs a yearly fee.
  3. That's it. It opens straight away and starts with Windows (turn that off in Settings > Display & window). New versions come by themselves.

Needs Windows 10 or 11 with .NET Framework 4.8, which Windows already includes. To remove it: turn off Start with Windows, quit, and delete the file.
